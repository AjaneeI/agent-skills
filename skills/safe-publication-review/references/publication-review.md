# Publication review record

Keep detailed evidence private when it contains sensitive material. A public review record must itself pass this review.

## Candidate

Destination/visibility; exact file hashes or commit range; source revision; authorized publication action; reviewer and actual method; date. Include generated files, new reachable history, PR text, and attachments. Omit unrelated private identifiers from the public summary.

## Privacy

Check personal or confidential information, credentials, private messages, personal paths/contact details, account identifiers, identifiable case histories, and combinations that reveal private context. Examples must be synthetic or explicitly cleared for public release. Clearance does not waive confidentiality, privacy, or redistribution rights. Inspect visual/document metadata where applicable.

Review every new commit, message, branch name, author/committer field, and PR surface. Preserve required public attribution without adding contact details by default. Do not upload raw private sources to external review services. Prefer an allowlisted export over an entire working directory. Record scan scope, contextual review, and uncertainty; no scan is a guarantee.

## Provenance

Distinguish original and reused content. Record upstream sources/versions, exact license terms, required notices, redistribution compatibility and unresolved rights. Publicly visible material is not necessarily reusable. Do not remove required attribution to simplify review.

## Quality

Check triggers, outcome, bounded authority, links, metadata, portability, examples, positive/negative cases, and limits. Distinguish format checks, static tests, text-only probes, actual client loading, observed tool behavior, separate review, and production use.

Professional presentation means accurate and generalizable, not hiding material technical defects. State unrun tests and limitations without publishing private personal narratives.

## Verdict

Ready for authorized publication / needs revision / blocked. Record blocking findings and resolutions, exact rechecked candidate, required independent review or its absence, and remaining unknowns. Add a publication receipt only after provider readback. Merge and activation are separate.
